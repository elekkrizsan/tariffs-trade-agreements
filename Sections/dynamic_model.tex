\section{Model}\label{sec:dynamic_model}

My approach draws from \textcite{amiti_whos_2020}, who study the effect of tariffs on prices and value over a 12-month period. They use a dynamic event study model with three-way high-dimensional fixed effects to identify how price and value elasticities in response to tariffs evolved in the 12 months following tariffs’ imposition. The model incorporates country-product, product-time, and country-time fixed effects, controlling for differences in products across countries, global product shocks, and country-specific macroeconomic shocks respectively. My model extends this framework, introducing a triple interaction to identify the moderating effect of trade agreement depth.

I first introduce a baseline model following \textcite{amiti_whos_2020} with the sole addition of a dummy for trade agreement depth. \footnote{See Table \ref{tab:model_variables} for variable descriptions.}
    \begin{align}
        \ln x_{ijt} = \; & \eta_{ij} + \delta_{jt} + \mu_{it} \notag \\
        + \; & \sum_{s = -\underline{T}}^{\overline{T}} \gamma_s \left( \mathbf{1}_{ijs} \times \ln \left( \frac{1 + \mathit{tariff_{ijs}}}{\mathit{1 +  tariff_{ij0}}} \right) \times \mathit{deep_{i}} \right) \notag \\
        + \; & \epsilon_{ijt}
    \end{align}

I restrict the sample to only the first three tariff waves, which applied generally, whereas subsequent Section 301 tariffs applied only to China, and extended to far more products than the initial three waves. As a result, the model can identify the impact of tariffs on a common set of country-product pairs. This restriction also permits the extension of the 12-month timeframe studied by \textcite{amiti_whos_2020} to 18 months. 

I then additionally control for bilateral interactions between the three interaction terms: time-tariff, time-depth, and tariff-depth. 
    \begin{align}
        \ln x_{ijt} = \; & \eta_{ij} + \delta_{jt} + \mu_{it} \notag \\
        + \; & \sum_{s = -\underline{T}}^{\overline{T}} \beta_s \left( \mathbf{1}_{ijs} \times \ln \left( \frac{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ijs}}}{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ij0}}} \right) \right) \notag \\
        + \; & \sum_{s = -\underline{T}}^{\overline{T}} \lambda_s \left( \mathbf{1}_{ijs} \times \mathit{deep_{i}} \right) \notag \\
        + \; & \phi \left( \ln \left( \frac{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ijs}}}{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ij0}}} \right) \times \mathit{deep_{i}} \right) \notag \\
        + \; & \sum_{s = -\underline{T}}^{\overline{T}} \gamma_s \left( \mathbf{1}_{ijs} \times \ln \left( \frac{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ijs}}}{\mathit{1 + tariff_{ij0}}} \right) \times \mathit{deep_{i}} \right) \notag \\
        + \; & \epsilon_{ijt}
    \end{align}

            $\delta_{jt}$ & Product-time fixed effect. & This absorbs time-varying forces that affect imports of a particular product across all countries (e.g. common technological change). \\[1ex]
